Privacy Policy — Draft

Draft for review — not yet a finalized privacy notice. TripFiver's legal operator, privacy contact, data retention periods, and third-party practices must be confirmed before public commercial launch.

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Information involved in the service

TripFiver provides travel planning and account features. Depending on how you use the service, information may include account email and authentication information, saved trips, destinations, travel dates, preferences, and itinerary requests. Technical and usage information may also be processed for operation, security, and troubleshooting.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, generate and save itineraries, show travel recommendations, operate the website, investigate technical issues, and protect the service.

Service providers and links

TripFiver currently uses third-party technology for account and trip storage, AI itinerary generation, and travel-related recommendations, including Supabase, OpenAI, Google Places, and hosting infrastructure. Travel booking links may lead to independent providers, including Expedia and Viator, which apply their own privacy practices. This list and the precise information shared with each provider require verification before launch.

AI travel requests

Trip details and preferences submitted for itinerary generation may be sent to an AI service to prepare travel suggestions. Do not enter passport numbers, payment card information, or other sensitive information into free-text trip requests.

Storage, retention, and your choices

Account and saved-trip information may be stored to make trips available across sessions. Retention periods, account deletion procedures, privacy request channels, cookie use, and applicable privacy rights will be documented in the finalized policy.

Contact and operator information

TripFiver is currently a project brand, not a representation that a corporation, LLC, or registered DBA exists. Verified operator and privacy contact information will be added before this policy is finalized.
