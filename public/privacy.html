Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TripFiver (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use our AI-powered travel planning website. TripFiver is a travel-planning service currently operated by an individual in Florida, United States, under the TripFiver brand. TripFiver is not currently represented as an LLC. TripFiver is not the airline, hotel, or activity provider for bookings made on third-party sites.

Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, account identifiers, and authentication-related information needed to sign in and maintain your account. Authentication is provided through Supabase; we do not ask you to put passwords in trip descriptions.
  • Travel planning information: destinations, departure locations, travel dates, budgets, group size, preferences, itinerary requests, generated plans, and saved trips.
  • Technical and usage information: information associated with requests, service errors, and security monitoring, such as network or device-related information that may be available to our hosting and technology providers.
  • Affiliate interactions: when you follow booking links, the booking provider or affiliate network may process referral identifiers and information about your interaction under its own policies.

How we use information

We use information to create travel plans, personalize suggestions, provide sign-in and saved-trip functionality, troubleshoot errors, secure the service, understand referral performance, and maintain and improve TripFiver.

AI and service providers

TripFiver uses technology providers to operate the service, including Supabase for account and trip data, OpenAI for AI-assisted itinerary generation, Google Places for location and travel-related suggestions, and hosting infrastructure for the website and APIs. Information relevant to a request may be transmitted to these providers to deliver the requested functionality. Their processing practices are also governed by their applicable policies and agreements.

Booking websites and affiliate links

TripFiver may link to independent travel providers, including Expedia and Viator. When you open a provider's website, that provider may collect information, set cookies, process payments, and handle reservations according to its own privacy policy. TripFiver may receive referral compensation for qualifying transactions. Viator experiences are currently in a testing environment and may not support real checkout.

Cookies and similar technologies

Authentication and other essential browser storage may be used to keep you signed in and operate the website. Third-party booking websites and embedded affiliate widgets may use their own cookies or similar technologies. Your browser settings may allow you to limit cookies, although some account features may then stop working.

Storage and retention

Saved trips and account information are retained to provide ongoing access to your plans. Information may also be retained as needed for security, operational, legal, or dispute-resolution purposes. We have not established a universal deletion period for every data category. You may request information about deleting your account or saved information through the contact channel below.

Security

We use access controls intended to separate users' saved trips and rely on service providers' security features. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Avoid entering passport numbers, payment card details, or other highly sensitive information into itinerary requests.

Your choices and privacy rights

You can review and manage saved trips within your account. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or other controls over your personal information. We will evaluate such requests under applicable requirements, subject to identity verification and legal exceptions.

International processing

Our technology providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live. Privacy protections may differ by location.

Children

TripFiver is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as TripFiver changes. The date above identifies the latest published revision. Material changes may be communicated through the website or another appropriate method.

Contact and operator details

For privacy questions or requests, use TripFiver's published support/contact channel when available. TripFiver is currently operated by an individual in Florida, United States. A dedicated privacy contact channel and any additional legally required operator disclosures must be confirmed before broad public launch.

Before broad public launch: TripFiver must confirm any legally required individual-operator identification, a working privacy contact, and data retention and provider practices. This policy describes the current service but has not been reviewed by legal counsel.
